## Meeting Minutes
### Folder: `Meeting Minutes/`
Contains multiple documents named after the dates of the weekly meetings.
- **Meeting Notes**: Summaries of discussions, action items, and feedback from each meeting.
- **Artifacts**: Weekly updates, progress reports, and any other relevant materials shared during the meetings.

## Intermediate Presentation

## Thesis Final
### Folder: `Thesis Final/`
This folder contains the final thesis materials and supporting documents.
- **Subfolders**:
  - **`Additional Documents/`**: Supplementary artifacts and resources that were helpful during the thesis.
  - **`Final Documents/`**: Contains the final PDF and LaTeX files of the thesis.
  - **`Images/`**: All the images used in the thesis document.
  - **`Literature Papers/`**: All papers referenced during the information extraction process.

### Files:
- **`Glossary.xlsx`**: A glossary of fairness-related concepts referenced in the thesis.
- **`Information_Extraction.xlsx`**: Document detailing the information extracted from surveyed literature papers.
